Multi-Layer Forensic Analysis

Uncover manipulation at
every layer

DeepGuard AI combines spatial, temporal, and semantic analysis to expose deepfake manipulation across multiple forensic dimensions simultaneously.

Face Detection & Alignment

Multi-scale face detection using MTCNN with landmark alignment. Precisely crops and normalizes faces for forensic analysis, supporting multiple faces per frame.

MTCNN 68 Landmarks Multi-face

CNN Feature Extraction

EfficientNet-B4 backbone with 17.5M parameters extracts deep spatial features from facial regions, capturing subtle texture inconsistencies and blending artifacts.

EfficientNet-B4 17.5M Params ImageNet Pretrained

Transformer Attention

Multi-head self-attention encoder with 4 layers and 8 attention heads models global spatial relationships across the entire face, detecting cross-region inconsistencies.

4 Encoder Layers 8 Heads 512-dim

Video Frame Analysis

Temporal consistency analysis across video frames with per-frame scoring, suspicious frame detection, and timeline visualization of manipulation probability.

Frame-level Timeline Chart MP4/AVI/MOV

Artifact Score Breakdown

Detailed scoring across four forensic dimensions: blending artifacts, texture inconsistency, eye reflection mismatch, and boundary edge noise.

4 Artifact Types Confidence Score Real-time

Explainable AI (XAI)

Grad-CAM visualizations and attention map overlays highlight exactly which regions the model focused on, providing interpretable forensic evidence for each decision.

Grad-CAM Attention Maps Interpretable
